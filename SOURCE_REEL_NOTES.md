Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZLBMRjRzMW/

Core workflow from the video:

1. Ask an AI agent what free AI skills would make sense for your business.
2. Build the skills.
3. Put them in a public GitHub repo.
4. Build a simple landing page for the pack.
5. Use a contact platform to find relevant people.
6. Email or DM the free pack.
7. Use reciprocity and useful free assets as the lead magnet.

Applied here for Lumenosis AI and real-estate operators.
