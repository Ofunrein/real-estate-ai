# Market Update Newsletter

Use this skill to turn raw market numbers into useful client-facing content.

## Inputs

Ask for:
- Market area and audience: buyers, sellers, investors, past clients, homeowners, renters.
- Stats: median price, average price, inventory, new listings, closed sales, pending sales, DOM, price reductions, months supply, mortgage rates.
- Timeframe and comparison period.
- Source names if available.
- Desired channel: email, blog, LinkedIn, Instagram carousel, SMS teaser, script.
- CTA: valuation, buyer consult, market call, newsletter signup, lead magnet.

## Workflow

1. Normalize the stats and label timeframes.
2. Identify the practical takeaway:
   - More leverage for buyers.
   - Inventory squeeze.
   - Seller price sensitivity.
   - Faster/slower market.
   - Segment divergence.
3. Explain what changed and why it matters.
4. Write for the audience, not for agents.
5. Include a clear CTA.
6. Add caveats and source/verification notes.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim prices will rise or fall with certainty.
- Do not give financial, legal, tax, or lending advice.
- Do not use fear-based language.
- Do not invent statistics or sources.
- If numbers conflict, call it out and explain what must be verified.

## Quality Bar

- Every statistic needs a plain-English meaning.
- Avoid generic "market is changing" copy.
- Make the CTA tied to the data: pricing review, home search reset, equity check, or neighborhood-specific report.
