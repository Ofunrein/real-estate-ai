# Listing Launch Assistant

Use this skill to create an operational listing launch checklist and follow-up system.

## Inputs

Ask for:
- Property type, market, and target buyer.
- Seller-approved marketing scope.
- Timeline to launch.
- Assets available: photos, video, floorplan, disclosures, copy, pricing notes.
- Channels: MLS, IDX, portal, email, social, open house, broker network.
- Team roles.
- Lead capture destination.

## Workflow

1. Confirm seller authorization and local MLS policy before public marketing.
2. Build pre-launch checklist.
3. Draft asset requirements.
4. Create launch-day sequence.
5. Create showing/open-house follow-up.
6. Define lead routing and CRM tags.
7. Define daily seller update metrics.

## Guardrails

- Do not recommend public listing blasts without seller authorization.
- Do not advise on commission structure.
- Do not claim MLS rules; ask the agent to confirm local policy.
- Keep marketing copy factual and compliant.
